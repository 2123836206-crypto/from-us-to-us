About the projectRoyal College of Art

A college with a memory, where those who came before welcome those arriving next.

From Us, To Us is a message exchange between departing and arriving students: a growing archive of practical guidance, reassurance and honest reflections.

Why letters?

Students arrive from around the world with different languages, cultures and experiences. An unfamiliar place can bring uncertainty, culture shock and, sometimes, isolation. A message from someone who has been there can make the invisible parts of college life easier to navigate.

How the exchange works

Students write or type what they wish they had known before arriving. They can use a name, a chosen signature or remain anonymous. Each letter is reviewed and shared with clear consent, then carried forward for a future student who may need it.

The archive is intended to grow across cohorts and campuses, preserving the timing, stories and small pieces of knowledge that formal handbooks often miss.
